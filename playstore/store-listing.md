## Full description

MadhvaNidhi brings daily Madhva practice into one calm, readable app, with portrait navigation and landscape content readers.

Choose Rigveda–Ashvalayana or Krishna Yajurveda–Taittiriya to read the complete
morning, noon and evening Sandhyavandana sequence. View tithi, paksha, masa,
nakshatra, yoga and karana in the Panchanga dashboard, with a selectable Madhva
Matha tradition and clear source labeling.

The offline devotional library includes mantras, Rigveda suktas, stotras,
namavalis, Vishnu Sahasranama, Rama Raksha, Venkatesha Suprabhata, Narasimha and
Krishna collections, Nitya Parayana, festival Puja procedures and Haridasa works.
Japa counting and Aayadi tools are included for daily practice.

Designed for large, legible Kannada text with a Material Design 3 light/dark interface and an orange accent.

Developed and published by VISWABHIRAM D HOLAGUNDI, an individual developer in Hosapete, Karnataka, India.

MadhvaNidhi is an independent devotional reference app. Matha names identify the
user's selected tradition; they do not imply endorsement. Official live data is
identified explicitly in the app.
