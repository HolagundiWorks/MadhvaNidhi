Privacy (summary)
-----------------
Sadhak profile and practice data are stored locally on the device (encrypted vault /
AES-GCM backup). There is no account sign-in and no cloud sync of the user vault.
Internet may be used for optional live Panchanga tradition data when selected and
reachable. The app does not sell personal data. See in-app Legal → Privacy for the
full declaration.
