## Structure of each mantra section (as printed)

Most mantra sections follow the same numbered order: maṅgalācaraṇa, prāṇāyāma, dēvatā dhyāna, āsana śuddhi, ṛṣyādi nyāsa, kara and aṅga nyāsa, dhyāna, japa count, dāna, japa upasaṃhāra, arghya pradāna. Use the numbered headings in the text, not this list, when checking a particular section.

## OCR caveats

- The Kannada OCR has many garbled characters, especially in headings, digits and Sanskrit diacritics. Several TOC items above were inferred from partial text.
- Check every mantra string against the printed book before any use. Items 4 (TOC line 206) and 7 (TOC line 210) need a clearer scan to confirm their names.
- Native-speaker or traditional review is needed before publication.
